Hardware Implementation of AES-128 Algorithm for Secure UART Communication

Under the guidance of Prof. Anita Bhatt • Birla Vishvakarma Mahavidyalaya

Implemented a hardware cryptographic subsystem integrating a 128-bit Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) cipher pipeline directly into serial UART transceivers.

  • Synthesized complete AES encryption and decryption engines (Key Expansion, SubBytes, ShiftRows, MixColumns, and Inverses) using Verilog HDL.
  • Integrated 128-bit SIPO and PISO conversion stages to bridge byte-oriented 8-bit UART streams with parallel 128-bit cryptographic states.
  • Demonstrated real-time dual-board communication across two Digilent Basys 3 (Xilinx Artix-7) FPGA boards, achieving low latency and secure end-to-end data transfer.
